My grandfather started with a single stove and a promise: nothing sits on the shelf past its day. That's still the rule. We make small batches, sell out most afternoons, and start again before sunrise. If a tray's gone, it's gone until tomorrow.

Every sweet on this counter is made with full-cream milk, real ghee and jaggery or sugar in the same proportions we've used since 1978 — no artificial shortcuts, whatever the season.
